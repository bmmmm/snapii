Notes on quartz

Quartz is a hard, crystalline mineral composed of silica. It is the second most abundant mineral in the continental crust of the earth, after feldspar.

Its crystals grow as six-sided prisms that end in six-sided pyramids, and many varieties are prized as gemstones, from amethyst to citrine and smoky quartz.

Piezoelectric quartz oscillators keep the time in nearly every wristwatch and computer, because a thin slice of the crystal vibrates at a very stable frequency.
